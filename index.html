Why CineSwin?

CineSwin is a student-built movie discovery and ticket booking platform. Our purpose is simple: make going to the movies easy, affordable and fun for everyone. We are guided by four values.

Convenience

Compare sessions and prices, then book from your phone or laptop in a single form.

Variety

From crime classics and sci-fi epics to animation and family comedies, there is something for every taste.

Accessibility

A clear layout, readable text and keyboard-friendly forms that work on any screen size.

Entertainment

Hand-picked films, including masterpieces from Christopher Nolan and Martin Scorsese.

Who is it for?

  • Families – G and PG picks everyone can enjoy together.
  • Couples – thoughtful, engaging films for date night.
  • Students – discounted student tickets and late sessions.
  • Movie enthusiasts – director-led collections and classics.

What we offer

Movie discovery

Browse every film with its genre, age rating, running time, price and session times. See all movies.

Smart recommendations

Tell us your favourite genre, age group and who you are watching with, and we will suggest the best matches. Try it.

Easy ticket booking

Choose a session, ticket type and payment method (voucher or card) in one simple form. Book tickets.
